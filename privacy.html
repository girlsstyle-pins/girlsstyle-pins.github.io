Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Girl's Style website and the Girl's Style Pin Planner app use, why, and how you can contact us about it.

Who we are

Girl's Style is a Pinterest account (@GirlsStyle_) that shares streetwear outfit ideas for women. The owner of that account runs this website and the Girl's Style Pin Planner app and is responsible for the data described here. Contact: krystianplotka255@gmail.com.

The Girl's Style Pin Planner app

Pin Planner is a private tool used only by the account owner to manage the owner's own Pinterest business account. It does not connect to, read or post to any other person's Pinterest account.

With the owner's permission, granted through Pinterest's own sign-in screen, the app accesses:

  • basic account information, such as the username and account type;
  • the account's boards and the Pins it has created;
  • analytics for the account and its Pins, such as impressions, saves and outbound clicks;
  • public trend data from Pinterest, where available.

How the app uses this data

  • To show the owner which Pins and topics perform well.
  • To draft new Pin titles, descriptions and images for the owner to review.
  • To publish or schedule a Pin only after the owner has reviewed and approved that specific Pin.

The app does not publish anything without the owner's approval of each individual Pin.

What is stored, and where

  • Pinterest access tokens are stored encrypted in private storage that only the owner can access.
  • Analytics are requested from the Pinterest API each time they are needed and are not kept after the analysis is finished.
  • The app keeps a record of the drafts it prepared (title, description, image, link and whether the owner approved it).

Services that process data for the app

  • Anthropic (Claude API) receives summaries of the account's analytics and draft texts to generate suggestions.
  • Telegram delivers drafts to the owner for approval.
  • GitHub hosts the app's code and runs its scheduled tasks, and hosts this website through GitHub Pages.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Visitors to this website

This website has no cookies, analytics or forms, and does not collect personal data. Our host, GitHub Pages, may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The page loads its font from Google Fonts.

Affiliate links

Some Pins and pages contain affiliate links, for example through the Awin network. When you click one, the store or network may set cookies to record that a purchase came from our link. Their own privacy policies apply to that data. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in Pinterest account settings. When the app is disconnected, its stored tokens and draft records are deleted.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to its processing. To use these rights, write to krystianplotka255@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
